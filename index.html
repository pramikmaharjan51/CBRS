About Our School

Rooted in love, care, and a commitment to academic excellence since 2039 B.S.

Our History & Philosophy

Canvas Basanta Ritu School (CBRS) was established in 2039 B.S. by a versatile and enthusiastic individual who aimed to cater to quality and extraordinary education that is affordable to children from almost every background.

Our philosophy is built on three pillars: Love, Care, and Quality Service. We believe that education is not just about textbooks, but about building character, fostering curiosity, and preparing students to be responsible global citizens.

The Basanta Ritu Metaphor

"Basanta Ritu" translates to the Spring season—a time of renewal, growth, and new beginnings. We see every child as a seed with unlimited potential, ready to bloom under the right guidance and care.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To create responsible and independent individuals who have the knowledge and attitude to be successful in an ever-changing world.

Mission: To develop one of the premier academic institutions in the region that prepares global citizens with the skills and attitudes required to accept the challenges of a fast-growing society.
